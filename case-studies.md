        <h3>Modernizing Heat Pump Control to Reduce Energy Costs</h3>

        <p>
          See how Blinky-Lite added electricity spot-price control to Autoseum’s existing heat pump system, switching between the heat pump and district heating without replacing the equipment. The solution saved the museum an average of approximately SEK 2,800 per day during the winter of 2022.
        </p>
