    <h2>Professional experience</h2>

    <h3>BL Monitor and Control AB</h3>
    <p><strong>Co-founder and CTO</strong> · Lund, Sweden · 2021–present</p>
    <p>
      Developing Blinky-Lite and integrating monitoring and control
      solutions for research and industry.
    </p>

    <h3>MAX IV Laboratory</h3>
    <p><strong>Accelerator Division</strong> · Lund, Sweden · 2018–2025</p>
    <p>
      Research Engineer in the RF Group and RF Group Leader.
    </p>

    <h3>European Spallation Source</h3>
    <p><strong>Accelerator Division</strong> · Lund, Sweden · 2011–2018</p>
    <p>
      Senior Accelerator Advisor, Chief Engineer and Radio Frequency
      Group Leader.
    </p>

    <h3>Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory</h3>
    <p><strong>Accelerator science and engineering</strong> · Batavia, Illinois, USA · 1988–2011</p>
    <p>
      Held scientific, engineering and leadership roles across the
      accelerator complex, including Associate Accelerator Division
      Head for Systems, Integration and Operations; Run II Upgrades
      Technical Manager; and department head positions in the
      Antiproton Source, Proton Source and Booster Synchrotron.
    </p>
    <p>
      Other work included Project X concept development, accelerator
      software, radio-frequency and microwave engineering, and
      instrumentation for the 21 cm project.
    </p>

    <h3>University of Wisconsin–Madison</h3>
    <p><strong>Postdoctoral Research Associate</strong> · Madison, Wisconsin, USA · 1987–1988</p>
    <p>Research in superconducting radio-frequency devices.</p>
