Blinky-Lite Instrumentation

Instrumentation for distributed equipment

Blinky-Lite Instrumentation is a family of compact, wireless modules—called Cubes—for measurement, monitoring and interlock status. Each Cube is designed to integrate with the Blinky-Lite control platform.

Install Cubes close to the sensors or equipment they serve, reducing the need for dedicated wired data connections to a central system. A common Raspberry Pi Pico W foundation, Wi-Fi and MQTT provide a consistent approach across the lineup.

Report conditions as they happen

Unlike a system that waits for the next polling interval, Blinky-Lite Cubes use publish-subscribe messaging to report measurements, state changes and alarm conditions when they occur. An out-of-tolerance reading can be sent promptly to Blinky-Lite, where it can trigger a notification or a configured machine-protection action.

This event-driven approach is especially valuable when equipment needs attention quickly. Response time and protection depend on the device configuration, network, and the complete protection system.
