    <h2>Practical control, built on experience</h2>

    <p>
      We develop and integrate Blinky-Lite, an open-source platform
      for monitoring and controlling equipment. Our aim is to help
      people see what is happening, respond to problems and access
      equipment remotely.
    </p>

    <p>
      Blinky-Lite is released under the MIT License. Customers can use
      and adapt the code, work with us on integration and support, or
      choose another approach that suits their needs.
    </p>

          European Spallation Source to help connect technology with
          real-world needs.
